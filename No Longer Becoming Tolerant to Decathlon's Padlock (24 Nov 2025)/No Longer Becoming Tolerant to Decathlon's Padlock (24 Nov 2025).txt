PREFACE

I asked the staff for padlocks, and someone directly took me to the area hanging a queue of padlocks in front of me. Nobody alerted me that their padlocks are slightly but critically different that their products also require the correct password for unlocking.

I did not know much of padlocks but I knew before that they shouldn't be requiring a correct password for locking only. I knew that Decathlon used to demonstrate some anti-humanity designs in their products but I had never thought of even a padlock incorporates the philosophy of such kind. After figuring out how to use it I realised that they simplified the design for which the correct password is both required for unlocking and locking.
