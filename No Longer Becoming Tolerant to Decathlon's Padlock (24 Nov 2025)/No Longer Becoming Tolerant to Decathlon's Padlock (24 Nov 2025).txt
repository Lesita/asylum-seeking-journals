No Longer Becoming Tolerant to Decathlon's Padlock
A SERIES OF MR. LI AO's ASYLUM-SEEKING JOURNEL

24 November 2025

===================================

DETAILS

During the last time I went to Decathlon for buying clothes and shoes, I also bought 2 padlocks, as I conspiracy to putting those 2 items together with my clothes and shoes purchasing in one receipt, that my cousin will be most likely to cover all the purchases for me. I asked the staff for padlocks, and someone directly took me to the area hanging a queue of padlocks in front of me. Nobody alerted me that their padlocks are slightly but critically different that their products also require the correct password for unlocking.

I did not know much of padlocks but I knew before that they shouldn't be requiring a correct password for locking only. I knew that Decathlon used to demonstrate some anti-humanity designs in their products but I had never thought of even a padlock incorporates the philosophy of such kind. After figuring out how to use it I realised that they simplified the design for which the correct password is both required for unlocking and locking.

I was disgusted, as Decathlon even roguely changed their design on even a tiny thing to make people annoyed. However, I still used it for tens of days, until on the 24th, I suddenly found that I forgot to lock my bag while having this padlock attached unlocked. This angered me, and after returning back to my rest place, I decided to destroy this evil thing by throwing heavily onto hard surfaces and hitting it with heavier objects. I destroyed one of them, and the opening was then locked simply by using strings of cloth, simply tighten and fasten it, as replacement. Although no longer a padlock is used, I gound relief that I had eliminated this nuisance.

My opinion was, as a manufacturer and trader of this category of commonly-used item the design could include innovations, but it must follow conventional principles despite these are not written documented. A conceptual change of an important design must be well-addressed with means of prominent, eye-catching dissemination of information. The practices of Decathlon has actually made customers to knowing this difference after purchase, which is very unethical and annoying.

OUTCOME

Despite the spending of the padlocks were covered by a relative I still have vowed to holding Decathlon accountable  This is a long-term effort.
